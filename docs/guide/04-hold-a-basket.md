# Hold a basket

A customer has gone to fetch their truck, or wants to add more before paying. You park their
basket, serve the next person, and pick it up again when they come back.

**Before you start:** the items must already be in the basket.

## Steps

### Parking a basket

1. With the basket on screen, press **Hold**.

2. Give it a name the counter will recognise — the customer's name, or what they are waiting
   for. "Mr Cruz — waiting on truck" is more use than "Hold 1".

3. Press to confirm.
   The basket clears and you can serve the next customer.

### Picking it up again

1. Press **Held baskets**.
   Every parked basket is listed with its name and total.

2. Find the one you want and press to recall it.
   The items come back onto the counter screen exactly as they were.

3. Carry on as normal — see [Ring up a sale](03-ring-up-a-sale.md) from step 5.

## What happens next

**Holding a basket does not reserve the stock.** The items stay on the shelf and anyone can
sell them. If a held basket sits too long and the stock goes, you will be told when you try to
take payment.

A basket can only be recalled once. If two counters try to pick up the same one, the second is
told it has already been taken — which is what stops the same order being rung up twice.

Held baskets belong to the warehouse they were created at. A basket held at Main does not
appear at Branch.

## If it does not work

| You see | It means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **This field is required** | the basket has no name | type a name and try again |
| nothing listed under **Held baskets** | nothing is held at this warehouse | check the warehouse box is the one you held it at |
| **Insufficient stock for the selected product and warehouse.** after recalling | the stock sold while the basket was parked | reduce the quantity, or sell from the other warehouse |
